Claims Status Enquiry

Purpose
Use this procedure when a policyholder or broker asks where a claim has got to. It covers motor and home claims.

Before you start
You will need the claim number. If the caller does not have it, ask for the policy number and date of loss instead.

Procedure
1. Log into Claims Central with your own account.
2. Search for the claim using the claim number.
3. If there is no such claim, tell the caller. This is common and is not an error.
4. Read the claim status and the outstanding amount.
5. If the claim is closed, do not reopen it. Pass the enquiry to the claims team with what you found.
6. If the outstanding amount is over $10,000.00 and the claim is older than 90 days, refer it to a team lead.
7. Otherwise, tell the caller the status and the outstanding amount.
8. Once the team lead has approved a referral, update the caller.

Never change the claim record or the payment details.
Calls about complaints go to the Complaints team, e.g. when the caller says they are unhappy with the handling.
